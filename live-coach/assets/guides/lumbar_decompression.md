# 5-Minute Lower Back Decompression Guide

**Target Focus:** Lumbar spine stiffness, posterior chain decompression, and pelvic reset.  
**Equipment Required:** Exercise mat or carpeted floor.  
**Intensity:** Very Low (Restorative).

---

## 1. Supported Child's Pose with Lateral Reach (60s)
- **Starting Position:** Kneel on the floor, toes touching, knees set wide apart.
- **Action:** Extend both arms forward onto the floor. Slowly sink your hips back toward your heels.
- **Cue:** Breathe deeply into your lower ribs. Gently walk your fingertips 3 inches to the right to open the left quadratus lumborum (QL), hold for 30s, then repeat on the opposite side.
- **Contraindication Notice:** Avoid if experiencing acute knee flexion discomfort; place a rolled towel behind the knee joint to reduce flexion angle.

---

## 2. Supine 90/90 Diaphragmatic Reset (90s)
- **Starting Position:** Lie flat on your back with calves resting elevated on a chair or couch, knees and hips bent at 90-degree angles.
- **Action:** Inhale slowly through the nose for 4 seconds, filling the lower abdomen. Exhale fully through pursed lips for 6 seconds.
- **Cue:** Feel the lower lumbar region naturally flatten and decompress against the ground without active muscular pressing.

---

## 3. Quadruped Cat-Camel (Gentle Range) (60s)
- **Starting Position:** Hands stacked directly under shoulders, knees stacked under hips.
- **Action:** Slowly articulate the spine into extension (lifting chest slightly, inhale) and flexion (tucking pelvis, spreading shoulder blades, exhale).
- **Cue:** Move only within pain-free active range. Do not force end-range lumbar hyperextension.

---

## 4. Supine Figure-Four / Piriformis Stretch (90s)
- **Starting Position:** Supine on back, both knees bent, feet flat on the floor.
- **Action:** Cross the right ankle over the left knee. Thread hands behind the left thigh and gently draw the knee toward your torso.
- **Cue:** Keep your head and shoulders relaxed on the ground. Hold 45s per side.

---

## Offline Safety Check
- If at any point numbness, tingling, or sharp pain radiates down the leg, immediately discontinue the movement and rest in supine position.
