# Knee-Sparing Lower Body Mobility Protocol

**Target Focus:** Patellofemoral decompression, quad tendon offloading, and hip mobility without deep knee flexion or high-impact ground reaction forces.  
**Intensity:** Low-impact restorative.

---

## 1. Terminal Knee Extensions (TKE) with Static Hold
- **Setup:** Anchor a light loop band at knee height around a sturdy post, or perform isometric straight leg quad sets supine.
- **Action:** Step inside loop with band placed behind knee crease. Step back slightly to create gentle tension. Straighten knee by engaging the VMO (vastus medialis) without hyperextending.
- **Reps/Duration:** 3 sets of 12 reps with 2-second isometric peak squeeze.
- **Benefit:** Quadriceps strengthening with zero shearing force on patellar tendon.

---

## 2. Spanish Squat Isometric Wall Hold (High Stance)
- **Position:** Back against wall, feet placed 18-24 inches forward, knees bent only to 45–60 degrees (never deep flexion).
- **Duration:** 4 sets of 30-second holds.
- **Benefit:** Analgesic isometric tendon loading proven to alleviate patellofemoral pain.

---

## 3. Glute Bridge Isometric
- **Position:** Supine, knees bent at 90 degrees, feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart.
- **Action:** Drive through heels, lift hips until shoulders, hips, and knees form a straight line.
- **Cues:** Keep hamstrings and glutes active; avoid arching lower back.
