# Rotator Cuff & Shoulder Protection Guide

**Target Focus:** Scapular stability, subacromial space preservation, rotator cuff activation.  
**Intensity:** Low.

---

## 1. Sidelying External Rotation
- Lie on side with elbow tucked tightly against rib cage at 90 degrees.
- Rotate forearm upward toward ceiling, keeping elbow pinned.
- 2 sets of 15 repetitions with slow eccentric tempo.

## 2. Prone Scapular Y-T-W Retractions
- Lie face down on floor with forehead supported.
- Form Y, T, and W positions with arms, lifting thumbs upward 2 inches while retracting shoulder blades without shrugging upper traps.
