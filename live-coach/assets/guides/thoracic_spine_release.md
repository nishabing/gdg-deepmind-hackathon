# Thoracic Spine Release & Posture Alignment Guide

**Target Focus:** Upper back extension, rib cage mobility, and cervical-thoracic junction relief.  
**Intensity:** Very Low (Restorative).

---

## 1. Quadruped Thread the Needle
- From hands and knees, thread one arm beneath the opposite armpit, resting the shoulder gently on the floor.
- Hold for 5 deep breaths per side.

## 2. Open Book Lateral Rotations
- Lie on side with knees bent at 90 degrees. Sweep top arm open across chest toward opposite floor, eyes following the hand.
- 10 smooth rotations per side.
