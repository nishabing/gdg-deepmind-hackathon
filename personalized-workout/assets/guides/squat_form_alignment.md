# Static Squat Form Alignment & Biomechanical Reference

**Target Focus:** Offline Squat Biomechanics, Joint Stacking, and Depth Self-Assessment.  
**Equipment Required:** Wall, sturdy chair or bodyweight.  
**Intensity:** Educational / Assessment.

---

## 1. Foot Stance & Tripod Pressure
- **Width:** Shoulder-width apart, toes flared outward 15–30 degrees according to hip socket anatomy.
- **Tripod Cue:** Maintain equal pressure across the 3 base points:
  1. Base of the big toe (1st MTP joint)
  2. Base of the pinky toe (5th MTP joint)
  3. Center of the heel
- **Check:** Ensure your medial arch does not collapse inward during descent.

---

## 2. Hip Hinge vs. Knee Travel
- Initiate movement by unlocking the hips backward slightly while allowing knees to track directly over the second and third toes.
- **Chest Position:** Maintain an active, proud chest with an approximate 45-degree forward torso incline matching your shin angle.

---

## 3. Depth & Lumbar Neutrality
- **Safe Working Depth:** Lower until hip crease is parallel with the top of the patella, or stop 2 inches above parallel if you observe a posterior pelvic tilt ("butt wink").
- **Knee Symptom Modification:** If anterior patellar pressure occurs, reduce depth to a high box squat (box height set at 90-degree knee angle) and cue posterior weight shift.
